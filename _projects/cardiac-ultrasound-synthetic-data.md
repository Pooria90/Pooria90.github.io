For my M.A.Sc. thesis at UBC, I developed ControlNet-based diffusion models to generate anatomically faithful synthetic echocardiograms. I evaluated the generated data through downstream segmentation, where augmenting U-Net models with synthetic images improved performance over real-only training, and explored CMMD as a metric aligned with downstream utility.

**Focus:** Biomedical Imaging · Ultrasound · Diffusion Models · Segmentation
