I am a Machine Learning Engineer at Railtown AI, where I work on agentic systems and applied ML engineering. My background is in biomedical engineering and medical imaging, with previous work on cardiac ultrasound segmentation, synthetic data generation with diffusion models, and MRI-based quantitative analysis.

I completed my M.A.Sc. in Biomedical Engineering at the University of British Columbia. My thesis focused on synthetic data augmentation for cardiac ultrasound segmentation using conditional diffusion models. Before Railtown, I worked at Prenuvo on medical imaging algorithms for quantitative MRI analysis.
